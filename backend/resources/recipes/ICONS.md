# Recipe icon sources

These marks identify the upstream applications; they do not imply endorsement. Upstream trademark policies still apply. Licences below apply to the separately sourced artwork, not to the rest of the panel. SVGs are source-form assets; normalization removes document declarations, editor metadata and unnecessary whitespace, retaining the mark. No external resources or embedded raster images ship. Ghost, Homepage, IT-Tools and Vaultwarden retain their official monochrome artwork over a rounded white backing so their dark parts remain legible in both panel themes.
